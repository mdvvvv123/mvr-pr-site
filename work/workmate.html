Background

A milestone worth maximising.

Workmate, a Singapore-based workforce management platform, was acquired by PERSOL Asia Pacific. We built a targeted campaign to maximise coverage and engagement for the milestone, with a strong press release, focused outreach and exclusive interviews with Workmate's CEO and key stakeholders.

The challenge

Turn one announcement into broad reach.

As a leading workforce platform in Southeast Asia, Workmate needed to maximise the impact of the acquisition among investors, partners and customers, with a strategy that ensured broad coverage and told the growth story clearly.

The approach

Release, outreach, exclusives.

The campaign ran on three components.

  • A compelling press release framing the deal and growth trajectory
  • Targeted outreach to the region's tech, business and startup media
  • Exclusive CEO and stakeholder interviews for depth
The results

49 placements across four markets.

The acquisition earned 49 placements across four countries and an estimated $1.8M in ad value.
