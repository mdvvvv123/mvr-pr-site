About

MVR is small on purpose. The person you meet is the person who runs your account.

We do PR that gets results. That is the whole job.

We started MVR because too many good brands were getting handed agency theatre: a generic release, a vanity hit, a junior who churns. We work the other way around.

We have landed over 2,000 placements across 94 countries, from a global venture firm to startups to the pet and food brands you see here. PR works the same in any of them: know the audience, earn the attention.

We spend more time reading a journalist's beat than writing the pitch. We build the relationships before we need them. And we are honest with you about what coverage can and cannot do, because managing that is half the job.

We treat your brand like our own, and we would rather show you than tell you. The case studies are the rest of the pitch.

Michaela VillaromanFounder, MVR Public Relations · Manila

Questions

How do you usually work with clients?
Most engagements run on a monthly basis so the work can build over time. We also take on defined projects, a launch or a single campaign, when that fits better.
What kind of brands do you work with?
Any industry. We have run PR for a global venture firm (Seedstars), a Philippine transportation brand (Siklesa), and the pet and food brands in our work, across tech, startups, and consumer. If you have something worth talking about, we can get it the right attention, whatever you sell.
What does it cost to get started?
Engagements start at a monthly rate scaled to your stage and goals. Tell us where you are and we will be straight about what is realistic for the budget.
Where are you based?
We are based in Manila and work with brands across the Philippines. Local media relationships and time on the ground are part of what you are hiring.
Can you guarantee coverage?
No one credible can. What we commit to is the strategy, the relationships, and the consistent effort that earns coverage. We are honest about what is realistic, every time.
How do we start?
Send a short note about your brand and where you want to be heard, using the form below or a direct email. We reply within one business day and, if it is a fit, we scope the first engagement from there.
