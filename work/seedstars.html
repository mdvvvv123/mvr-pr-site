Background

A single brand story, told in five regions.

Through strategic storytelling and market adaptability, we secured global media coverage in prestigious outlets like TechCrunch, Bloomberg and CNN, establishing Seedstars as a prominent player in the early-stage startup ecosystem and supporting the growth of its portfolio companies.

Over the period, the work drove roughly a 186% increase in media mentions.

The challenge

Five media landscapes, no local agencies.

The challenge was continuously and simultaneously establishing the company's presence in Africa, Asia, Europe, North America and South America. Each region brought its own obstacles, from varied media landscapes to language barriers and cultural differences.

With limited resources and no option to hire local PR agencies in each country, we had to prioritise efforts and maximise impact from a single desk.

The approach

Targeted storytelling, region by region.

We navigated diverse media landscapes to secure high-quality placements through research, tailored pitching and genuine relationships.

  • Thorough research into each market and its press
  • Compelling pitches tailored to each region
  • Genuine, lasting relationships with journalists
  • One consistent global brand narrative across countries
The results

2,000+ placements, 94 countries, five regions.

The work earned more than 2,000 placements across 94 countries and five regions, an estimated $262M+ in ad value, and roughly a 186% increase in media mentions.
